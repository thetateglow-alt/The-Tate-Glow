Exchange & Refund Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · The Taté Glow

At The Taté Glow, we are committed to providing jewelry that offers enduring luxury, exquisite craftsmanship, and effortless daily comfort. If an item does not completely delight you or requires an alternative size, our concierge team is here to assist you through our transparent, reassuring policy.

14-Day Window Exchanges and returns can be initiated within 14 days of order delivery.
Pristine Condition Items must be unused, unworn, and preserved in their original luxury packaging.
48h Defect Care Complimentary courier exchange if any transit damage or error occurs.

1. Exchange Policy

If you wish to exchange a piece for another size, finish, or design, we gladly facilitate exchanges subject to the following criteria:

  • Customers can request an exchange within 14 days of receiving their order.
  • Items must be unused, unworn, and in their original packaging.
  • Products must be returned in their original condition, with all tags, protective seals, pouches, and accessories included.

2. Refund Policy

Should you prefer a refund rather than an exchange, your request will be honored under our standard terms:

  • Refund requests can be submitted within 14 days of receiving an order.
  • Refunds are only accepted for eligible items that meet all our return and inspection conditions.
  • Once the returned item is inspected and approved by our quality control team, the refund will be processed through the original payment method (or bank transfer/wallet for Cash on Delivery).
  • Refund processing time may take 5–10 business days depending on your financial institution or card issuer.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Items

Every piece undergoes stringent multi-point inspection before dispatch. In the exceptional case an order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect:

Important: Please contact customer support within 48 hours of delivery and provide your order number along with clear photos of the issue and outer packaging.

Upon confirmation, we arrange a priority courier to collect the damaged piece and dispatch a pristine replacement at zero additional charge.

4. Non-Returnable Items

To ensure strict hygiene and guarantee authentic quality for every client, the following are non-returnable:

  • Used or worn accessories: Any item showing scratches, residue, cosmetic traces, perfume scent, or wear.
  • Items damaged due to misuse: Damage resulting from accidental force, improper storage, or unauthorized modification.
  • Incomplete packaging: Products returned without original pouches, boxes, or included accessories.

5. How to Request an Exchange or Refund

Initiating a request is simple. Follow these three effortless steps:

Step 01
Contact Concierge

Email our concierge team with your order number and the reason for your request.

Step 02
Courier Scheduling

Our team reviews your details and coordinates a courier collection from your doorstep.

Step 03
Inspection & Resolution

Once inspected, your exchange piece is dispatched or refund credited within 5–10 business days.

6. Important Information & Shipping Fees

Return Shipping Costs: For preference-based exchanges and standard returns, return courier shipping costs and any applicable collection fees will be communicated to the customer and agreed upon before the return is arranged.

If the exchange or return is due to an error on our part or a proven product defect, all collection and replacement shipping fees are covered entirely by The Taté Glow.
