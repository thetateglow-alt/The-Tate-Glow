Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Who We Are

Welcome to Taté Glow (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We are an online jewelry boutique offering hypoallergenic, tarnish-free jewelry crafted from pure surgical stainless steel.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at:
Email: thetateglow@gmail.com

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect information to provide, fulfill, and elevate your shopping experience. This includes:

  • Order & Delivery Information: Your full name, delivery address (city, street, building details), telephone number, and payment method choice (Cash on Delivery or Card).
  • Contact Information: Your email address for sending order receipts, courier updates, and customer support communications.
  • Order History: Information regarding the jewelry pieces you purchase (item, size, price, date, and order reference numbers).
  • Google Account Information: If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information provided by Google Authentication (such as your display name, email address, and profile photo URL).
  • Guest Checkout Information: If you shop as a guest without creating an account, we collect only the necessary delivery and contact details required to fulfill and dispatch your order.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data strictly for legitimate ecommerce operations, specifically:

  • Processing, packaging, and dispatching your jewelry orders via our trusted courier delivery network across Egypt.
  • Sending itemized order confirmation receipts, jewelry care certificates, and delivery updates.
  • Maintaining your order history and loyalty Glow Points balance so you can redeem discounts on future purchases.
  • Providing customer service and responding to requests, inquiries, or feedback.
We Never Sell Personal Data: Taté Glow does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to advertisers or any third-party marketing companies under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

To deliver a smooth and secure digital experience, we partner with reliable infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure user authentication (Google Sign-In, Email/Password, and anonymous guest sessions) and our secure cloud database (Firestore) to persist order records.
  • EmailJS: Used to dispatch automated, itemized order confirmation receipts directly to your email inbox.
  • Courier & Delivery Partners: Your name, phone number, and delivery address are shared with our licensed couriers solely for delivering your package.

5. Guest Checkout Data

When you choose “Continue as guest”, we do not require a password or permanent account. The contact and delivery data you supply at checkout is used exclusively to fulfill that specific order and communicate shipping updates. If you later choose to create an account or sign in with Google, your guest orders can be linked to your permanent profile.

6. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, SSL/TLS data transmission, and strict Firebase security rules to safeguard your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We retain order records only for as long as necessary to comply with legal, tax, and accounting requirements.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You have the right to access, inspect, rectify, or request the permanent deletion of your personal data stored with us at any time. To exercise these rights, please send an email from your registered address to:

thetateglow@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Request - [Your Name]”. We will process and confirm your request promptly.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our operations or applicable regulations. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated “Last updated” timestamp.
